Why we’re called khyzr.

الخضر

Al-Khidr — “the Green One.”

  1. The one they sought

    In old stories from this part of the world, Al-Khidr is the wise man travelers went looking for when they couldn’t see the way forward.

  2. Never the ruler

    He wasn’t a king and didn’t want to be one. He gave counsel, did the work, and moved on — the credit stayed with the people he helped.

  3. The green trace

    They say grass grew wherever he stood. We took the name as a standard to hold ourselves to: when we leave, things should be growing that weren’t before.

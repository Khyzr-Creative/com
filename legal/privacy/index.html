What reaches other servers

Opening a page sends a request to our hosting provider, which records it in its server logs, as the host of any website does: your IP address, the page you asked for, the time, and which browser you used. That is how the page reaches you and how the service is kept running. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you.

One other service is asked for files while a page loads: Google Fonts, for the typefaces. Your browser fetches them from Google’s servers directly, so Google receives your IP address and browser details with the request, and handles them under its own privacy policy.

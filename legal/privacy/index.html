Khyzr Consulting Group (“khyzr,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the websites www.khyzr.com and www.khyzr.ai (collectively, the “Sites”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our Sites or services, you agree to the practices described here.

1 Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to your inquiry or deliver our services.

Contact form submissions
When you reach out through our site, we collect your first name, last name, email address, company name, area of interest, and the message you provide.
Khai AI chat interactions
Conversations with Khai, our AI assistant on khyzr.ai, are processed in session and used to provide a contextual response. We do not permanently store individual chat transcripts linked to your identity.
Technical data
Standard server logs may capture your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used for security and operational purposes only.

2 How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and initiate a consulting engagement
  • To operate and improve the Sites and the Khai AI assistant
  • To protect the security and integrity of our platforms
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3 AI-powered services (Khai)

khyzr.ai hosts Khai, our AI consulting assistant powered by large language model technology. When you interact with Khai, your messages are sent to our API server and processed to generate a response. Sessions are maintained temporarily to provide conversational context and are not used to build permanent user profiles.

Khai’s responses are informational in nature and do not constitute formal consulting advice, legal counsel, financial guidance, or medical opinion. Khyzr Consulting Group retains editorial control over Khai’s system configuration and behavior.

4 Cookies and tracking

Our Sites use minimal, functional cookies necessary for core site operation. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. If this changes, this policy will be updated and visitors will be notified.

5 Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or fulfill the purposes described above. We do not archive personal data beyond active client engagements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below.

6 Third-party services

Our Sites are hosted on infrastructure provided by third-party cloud providers. We may use service providers to operate our email, calendar booking, and AI infrastructure. These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only as directed by khyzr. We do not share identifying information with our strategic partners (NPC, Texas FinServ, 96 Meridian AI, WaveAxis, TD SYNNEX, or Unique Creations) without your explicit consent.

7 Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@khyzr.com.

8 Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your data to the best of our ability.

9 Children

Our Sites and services are intended for business professionals and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of our Sites after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
